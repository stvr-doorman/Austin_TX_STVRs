Austin map: what the colors mean

Green areas contain parcels in an address block listed in Austin’s license export. They do not identify the licensed unit, establish the owner, or authorize every parcel in the block.

⚠ means no license number was detected in saved listing text. A detected number can be stale, incorrect, or outside this snapshot. Nearby parcels and automated host/nickname matches remain unverified.

Parcel addresses and prior-year owner fields use a December 2025 Travis County snapshot. This source covers Travis County; Austin portions in other counties may have no parcel coverage. This independent community tool does not determine legal compliance.
